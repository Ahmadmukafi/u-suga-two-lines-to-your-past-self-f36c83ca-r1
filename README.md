A two-line warning to my past self: never trust the printer on the morning of a demo — and what that one jam changed forever.

- Line 1 warns about one specific moment: the printer jamming on page one, in front of everyone, while the client watches.
- Line 2 traces the cause and effect: the jam taught me to print the backup the night before, so I never walk into a demo unprepared again.

Entry: `main.ts` writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (241 characters, no trailing newline).
